# FocusAI

## Vision

FocusAI is an AI-powered deep work platform designed to help students and professionals achieve their highest level of focus while maintaining a healthy balance between productivity and well-being.

Unlike traditional Pomodoro timers or task management apps, FocusAI combines artificial intelligence, distraction protection, adaptive focus sessions, gamification, analytics, and mental wellness into a single intelligent productivity system.

Our mission is to help users work smarter, avoid burnout, and build lifelong deep work habits.
